{%- comment -%} A cover, drawn rather than photographed. Used wherever a card, a list or a share image needs a picture and the entry did not provide one. Nothing is fetched and nothing is built: an inline SVG, made from the entry's collection. It is a SOLID GROUND and a MARK, and nothing else. No title — that is already under the card in real type that wraps and can be selected, and setting it again inside a picture means it is set twice, at a size nobody chose, clipped the moment the card is narrower than the artwork. No gradient, no pattern, no corner glow either: three treatments competing on a picture whose whole job is to say what KIND of thing this is. The colour says the collection; the mark says the rest. The file-based twin is tools/thumbs.mjs (`npm run thumbs`), which draws the same picture as a real .svg for the cases that need a URL. Params: item (required) size (kept for callers; the art is the same at every size) {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign it = include.item -%} {%- assign label = it.collection | default: "pages" -%}
