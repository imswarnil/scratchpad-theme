## Why cut a trailer first

A trailer is a forcing function. If ninety seconds will not hold, the
twenty minutes will not either — and finding that out before the edit is
three weeks old is the cheapest note you will ever get.

This one went through eleven passes. The first nine were about pace. The
last two were about whether the voice-over was needed at all; it was not.
