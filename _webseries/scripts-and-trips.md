## Why a series and not a film

A single film has to decide what the trip *meant* before the edit is finished.
A series does not: each episode can be honest about the part of the route it
covers, and the meaning is allowed to arrive in the last one.

Shot on one body, two primes, and a recorder — the whole kit fits in a daypack,
which is the constraint the series is really about.
